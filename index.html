Meet Your Personal Trip Designer 🏰

I’m Staci, and I unapologetically claim Cinderella’s Castle as my primary residence.

While I’ve officially been designing bespoke travel experiences for three years, my true resume is built on the ground. With 19 personal trips to Walt Disney World under my belt, I don't just know the parks—I know how to master them. My heart will always belong to the Magic Kingdom (Cinderella is my favorite, after all!), but my real superpower is park strategy.

How strategic? I once successfully conquered five theme parks in a single day. Yes, five. That even includes a highly classified, strictly off-the-record detour over to Islands of Adventure (shh... don't tell the Mouse).

From coordinating multi-generational 60th birthday celebrations to designing unforgettable, family-focused milestone trips—including my own upcoming 40th birthday celebration with my kids—I know exactly what it takes to elevate a vacation from stressful to spectacular.
